Quick Start Guide

1. Risk Assessments

Navigate to the 'New' tab to create a new risk assessment. Fill in the hazard details, select the severity and likelihood, and download the generated PDF report.

2. Reporting Observations & Incidents

Use the 'Observe' or 'Incident' tabs to log events. You can assign corrective actions and track them to completion.

3. Inspections

Go to the 'Inspect' tab to fill out a facility checklist. Failed items will automatically generate corrective actions.

4. Admin & Users

If you are an Administrator, use the 'Admin' tab to manage facilities and invite new users to your organization.
